I'm a video editor and multimedia creative with a focus on shaping story through pacing, rhythm, and visual flow. My background in music influences how I approach editing, helping me bring emotional timing and impactful sound design to my projects.

I've worked across a range of projects, from self-produced social content to brand and creative work, covering the full process from concept to final delivery. I'm especially drawn to edits that feel intentional, immersive, and keep the viewer engaged from start to finish.
